# Transactional email: shortlist (Sam, 12 Oct)

For password resets, receipts and booking confirmations. About 3,000 emails a month now,
maybe 10,000 next year.

## Mailcrate
- £9/month up to 10,000 emails
- Hosted in their US data centres
- Excellent deliverability reputation, good templates, nice dashboard

## Postbird
- £14/month up to 10,000 emails
- EU region available (Frankfurt), selectable at sign-up
- Simple API, decent templates, UK-based support

## Sendwell
- About £1/month at our volume (pay per email)
- EU region available
- Runs on top of AWS: you connect your own AWS account and set up IAM

My lean: Mailcrate. Cheapest of the two easy ones and the best deliverability.
